Questions

Good to know

Is it safe to print my IBAN as a QR code?

Yes. The QR only stores your IBAN in the State Bank of Pakistan's standard Raast format — the exact same IBAN you already share to receive transfers. Nobody can withdraw money or access your account with an IBAN.

Which banks and wallets are supported?

Every Pakistani bank and licensed wallet that supports Raast P2P IBANs — including SadaPay, JazzCash, easypaisa, Meezan Bank, HBL, UBL, MCB, Bank Alfalah, Allied Bank, Faysal Bank, Askari Bank, NBP, and more. Even if a bank's 4-letter code isn't in our label list, the generated Raast QR still works normally.

What exactly is encoded inside the QR?

A standard static Raast P2P string: format header (000202), static indicator (010211), scheme code (020200), your 24-character IBAN under tag 04, and a 4-character CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE checksum under tag 10 so scanning apps can verify it wasn't misread. The amount is left open for the customer to type.

Any security tips for shop counters?

Print the card at least 8–10 cm wide, laminate it or place it behind glass, and glance at it occasionally to make sure nobody has pasted a different QR sticker on top of yours.

How do I find my IBAN?

Open your bank or wallet app and look under "Account details", "My account" or "IBAN". It's also printed on your cheque book and bank statement, and any branch can tell you. A Pakistani IBAN is 24 characters and starts with PK.

I only have my account number. Can I get my IBAN?

Yes. Use the free converter: pick your bank, type your account number, and it calculates the IBAN and opens it here as a QR. Some banks add branch codes, so always confirm the IBAN in your bank app or scan the QR and check your name before printing. Open the IBAN converter →
